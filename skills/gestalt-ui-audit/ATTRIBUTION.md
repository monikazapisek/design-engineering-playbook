# Attribution and bibliography

The principles in this skill come from perception research. The structure, wording, test IDs,
scoring scale, procedure, conflict rules, and worked example are original work licensed under MIT
(see `LICENSE`). The underlying principles and findings belong to the authors below.

## Bibliography

### Foundational

- **Wertheimer, M. (1923).** Untersuchungen zur Lehre von der Gestalt. II. *Psychologische
  Forschung, 4*, 301–350. Source of proximity, similarity, continuity (good continuation), and
  common fate.
- **Rubin, E. (1915).** *Synsoplevede Figurer.* Copenhagen: Gyldendal. Source of the figure/ground
  distinction.
- **Köhler, W. (1929).** *Gestalt Psychology.* New York: Liveright.
- **Koffka, K. (1935).** *Principles of Gestalt Psychology.* New York: Harcourt, Brace. Closure and
  the law of Prägnanz as used here.

### Later principles and evidence

- **Palmer, S. E. (1992).** Common region: A new principle of perceptual grouping. *Cognitive
  Psychology, 24*(3), 436–447.
- **Palmer, S. E., & Rock, I. (1994).** Rethinking perceptual organization: The role of uniform
  connectedness. *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 1*(1), 29–55. https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03200760
- **Quinlan, P. T., & Wilton, R. N. (1998).** Grouping by proximity or similarity? Competition
  between the Gestalt principles in vision. *Perception, 27*(4), 417–430.
  https://doi.org/10.1068/p270417
- **Wagemans, J., Elder, J. H., Kubovy, M., Palmer, S. E., Peterson, M. A., Singh, M., & von der
  Heydt, R. (2012).** A century of Gestalt psychology in visual perception: I. Perceptual grouping
  and figure–ground organization. *Psychological Bulletin, 138*(6), 1172–1217.
  https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029333

## What this skill does not contain

- No passages, figures, or examples copied from any source or practitioner article.
- No research-based spacing ratio. The 1.5× proximity threshold is a working value and is labelled
  as one wherever it appears.
- No claim that any principle always beats another.

## Verification status

On 2026-10-14, publisher or index records confirmed the journal, volume, issue, pages, and DOI of
Palmer 1992, Palmer & Rock 1994 (Springer), Quinlan & Wilton 1998 (SAGE), and Wagemans et al. 2012
(author copy of the published article). Palmer 1992's claim that common region can override
proximity and similarity, and Quinlan & Wilton's finding that colour and proximity bind strongly
while shape binds weakly, were checked against the abstracts. The foundational works are cited at
book or article level; no page numbers are claimed.
